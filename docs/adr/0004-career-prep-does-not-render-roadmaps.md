# Career Prep does not render Roadmaps

A Roadmap is a standalone written reference — role description, topic sequence, timeline — read end to end by anyone who wants the map, and Career Prep is the same subject matter in a different medium: explanation, practice, checkpoint, XP. Career Prep therefore owns its own structure (Journey → Stage → Topic) and takes the Roadmap only as an *authoring input*: the author reads the AI Engineering Roadmap and creates the Topics for the AI Engineering Journey, which is why the two look alike, but nothing at runtime reads a Roadmap to build a Journey. A Stage may link out to a Roadmap for the learner who wants the full map; it renders fine without one.

This reverses the Roadmap/Track merge recorded in ticket 03 of the Career Prep map, where a Roadmap Step gained attached Questions and a Checkpoint and the Roadmap page carried progress. That coupling meant a Checkpoint was two scrolls from the material it tested, Career Prep's sequence could not be edited without editing a Roadmap document, and Step identity — parsed from markdown headings — became load-bearing for grading and certification. The considered alternative was to keep the merge and simply hide the progress UI; it was rejected because the dependency, not the widget, was the problem.

**Consequences.** Steps stop carrying Questions, Checkpoints and progress, so the 194 Checkpoints keyed to `(roadmap_id, step_slug)` are remapped to Topics and the 184 generated placeholders — which existed only to give every Step one — are deleted rather than migrated. `step_progress` is left in place with its real learner history intact but is no longer written to; `topic_progress` replaces it, and `grade_topic_checkpoint()` verifies that the submitted question really is that Topic's Checkpoint before marking anything complete. The cost is duplication of subject matter between a Roadmap document and its Journey's Topics, kept deliberately: they are written for different purposes and are allowed to diverge.
