# 12 — Prototype: public profile and certificate

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

The public profile and the certificate are acquisition surfaces, not just rewards. Prototype both.

- `/u/<username>`: contribution calendar, completed Roadmaps, badges, certificates, XP/level. What is deliberately absent?
- Does it read as credible to a hiring manager who has never heard of this site? That is the actual bar.
- The share affordance — copy link, LinkedIn, CV.
- The certificate itself: what it looks like, and whether it is an image, a page, or both.
- `/verify/<id>`: the public verification page a stranger lands on. It must be obviously authoritative and load without auth.
- The opt-out state — what a private profile shows to a visitor.
- The timed final assessment entry and result screens.

Constraints: existing tokens only. Use the verification and LinkedIn findings from ticket 02. Link the prototype from this ticket.
